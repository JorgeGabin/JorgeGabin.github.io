Jorge Gabín

information retrieval · nlp · machine learning

I am an associate professor at the Universidade da Coruña and a software engineer at Linknovate, a platform for tracking innovation and searching for technologies.

My research is in information retrieval, natural language processing and machine learning. I defended my PhD in computer science in 2025, part of it spent visiting the Terrier Group at the University of Glasgow. The other half of my week goes into building the search products at Linknovate, which keeps the research honest.

If you are a student looking for a final-year or master's project in retrieval, write to me.
